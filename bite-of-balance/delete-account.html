Delete your Bite of Balance account

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Bite of Balance app (published by Apostolos Agrafas on Google Play and the App Store), what is deleted when you do, and what is kept.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Bite of Balance and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete account to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. This cannot be undone: to use the app again you would have to sign up as a new client and be approved by the clinic again.

2. Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email tolisatolis7@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Write from the email address of the Google, Facebook, Instagram or Apple account you signed in with, or include your full name and phone number so we can find your record. We delete the account and confirm by email within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

Deleted immediately, whichever way you ask:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender and height
  • Your sign-in account and its link to your Google, Facebook, Instagram or Apple account
  • Notes your dietitian kept about you

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Your weight and body measurements, meal plans and appointment history remain in the clinic's records under an anonymous entry ("Deleted user"). They are no longer linked to your name or any other identifying detail.
  • If you want these records erased as well, say so in your email request (or email us after deleting in the app). They are deleted within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as appointment and billing records, are kept for up to 5 years after deletion, as set out in our Privacy Policy, and then removed.

5. Questions

For anything about your account or your data, email tolisatolis7@gmail.com.
